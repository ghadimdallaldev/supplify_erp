## 1. Purpose

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the agreement between Supplify and a business tenant where Supplify processes personal data or business data on behalf of that tenant.

## 2. Roles

For tenant-controlled business data, the tenant is generally the data controller or equivalent business owner, and Supplify acts as a processor/service provider to provide the platform.

For Supplify account, security, billing, analytics, admin, and platform operations, Supplify may act as an independent controller or equivalent.

## 3. Processing Instructions

Supplify will process tenant data to provide, secure, support, maintain, and improve the platform, and as otherwise documented in the Terms, Privacy Policy, and product settings.

## 4. Categories of Data

Processed data may include user data, staff data, driver data, guest reservation data, restaurant/supplier contact data, order data, invoice data, delivery data, chat data, product data, uploaded files, audit logs, and technical data.

## 5. Security Measures

Supplify will use reasonable technical and organizational measures including access controls, authentication, permission checks, logging, backups, secure infrastructure, admin access controls, and incident response procedures.

## 6. Subprocessors

Supplify may engage subprocessors for hosting, storage, authentication, messaging, email, WhatsApp/SMS, push notifications, payments, analytics, support, monitoring, AI, and other services. Supplify remains responsible for subprocessors used to provide the platform.

## 7. Confidentiality

Supplify personnel and contractors with access to tenant data must be subject to confidentiality obligations or professional duties of confidentiality.

## 8. Admin Access and Impersonation

Supplify may access tenant data or impersonate tenant accounts for support, debugging, security, billing, and compliance. Such access may be logged and limited to authorized personnel.

## 9. Data Subject Requests

Where a person submits a request relating to tenant-controlled data, Supplify may direct the request to the relevant tenant or assist the tenant where reasonably possible.

## 10. Deletion and Export

Upon termination or request, Supplify may provide export or deletion assistance where technically feasible and legally permitted. Supplify may retain data in backups, audit logs, billing records, legal records, or security records as needed.

## 11. Incident Notification

Supplify will notify affected tenants of confirmed security incidents involving tenant data where required by applicable law or where Supplify determines notification is appropriate.

## 12. Audits

Supplify may provide reasonable information about security and data processing practices. On-site audits require prior written agreement and may be subject to confidentiality, security, and cost limitations.

## 13. International Transfers

Tenant data may be processed or stored outside Lebanon. Supplify will use reasonable safeguards where required.

## 14. Conflict

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms, this DPA controls only for data processing matters.
