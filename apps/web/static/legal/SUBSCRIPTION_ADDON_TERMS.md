## 1. Scope

These Terms govern Supplify subscriptions, Free Trial, paid plans, add-ons, billing, upgrades, downgrades, account locks, cancellation, and refunds.

## 2. Plans

Supplify may offer Free Trial, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Enterprise, or other plans. Plans may differ by tenant type, features, limits, users, branches, warehouses, storage, order limits, deal limits, support level, and available tools.

## 3. Free Trial

The Free Trial is a time-limited evaluation plan and is not a permanent free production plan. It may include broad demo access with low limits. After expiry, accounts may become read-only until upgraded or extended.

## 4. Billing

Subscriptions may be billed monthly, yearly, manually, by invoice, or through a payment provider. Taxes, fees, exchange rates, and payment provider charges may apply.

## 5. Renewal

Paid subscriptions may renew automatically unless cancelled according to the applicable process. Failure to pay may result in account restrictions.

## 6. Account Locks

Supplify may lock or restrict accounts for expired trials, overdue payments, cancelled subscriptions, failed payments, chargebacks, fraud risk, or policy violations. Read-only access may remain available at Supplify’s discretion.

## 7. Plan Limits

Plan limits may include users, branches, warehouses, orders per day, SKUs, storage, chats, quick lists, supplier deals, active promotions, reservations, and other meters. Supplify may block actions when limits are reached.

## 8. Add-ons

Supplify may offer paid add-ons for additional branches, warehouses, storage, features, support, or usage. Add-ons may increase effective limits for a tenant.

Add-ons may be manually granted by an admin until automated billing is available. Manual grants may still represent paid commercial commitments between Supplify and the tenant.

## 9. Branch and Warehouse Add-ons

Branches are valuable business locations and may require paid add-ons after included plan limits. Warehouses are supplier operational locations and may have separate add-on pricing.

More than a configured number of branches may require Enterprise or contact-sales approval.

## 10. Upgrades and Downgrades

Upgrades may take effect immediately or at the next billing period. Downgrades may restrict new creation while existing records remain readable. Downgrades may require reducing usage or purchasing add-ons.

## 11. Over-Limit Accounts

If a tenant exceeds a new or downgraded limit, Supplify may allow existing data to remain readable while blocking creation of additional resources until the tenant upgrades, buys add-ons, or receives an override.

## 12. Refunds

Unless required by law or expressly agreed, subscription and add-on fees are non-refundable for periods already used. Supplify may offer credits or extensions at its discretion.

## 13. Cancellation

Cancelling a subscription may prevent future renewal but does not automatically delete data. Access may continue until the end of the paid period or become restricted depending on the plan and payment status.

## 14. Enterprise

Enterprise plans may require custom pricing, manual approval, contract terms, custom support, custom limits, and administrative assignment.

## 15. Changes

Supplify may change plan features, pricing, limits, and add-ons for future billing periods. Material changes may be communicated where appropriate.
