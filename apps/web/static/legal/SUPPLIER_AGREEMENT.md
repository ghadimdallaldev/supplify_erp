## 1. Scope

This Supplier Agreement applies to suppliers using Supplify to list products, receive orders, manage catalogs, fulfill deliveries, issue invoices, create deals, purchase boosts for sponsored placement, manage warehouses, manage drivers, and interact with restaurants.

## 2. Supplier Status

The supplier is an independent business. Supplify provides software tools and is not the seller of the supplier’s products unless expressly agreed in writing.

## 3. Product Listings

Suppliers must ensure product names, SKUs, descriptions, units, images, packaging, allergens, specifications, availability, prices, discounts, and delivery terms are accurate and lawful.

Suppliers must promptly update products that are out of stock, unavailable, discontinued, expired, restricted, or incorrectly priced.

## 4. Pricing and Price Lists

Suppliers are responsible for all prices shown to restaurants, including customer-specific pricing, discounts, bulk pricing, temporary pricing, and taxes. Suppliers must honor accepted orders unless a lawful correction or cancellation applies.

## 5. Inventory, Warehouses, and Availability

Suppliers must keep stock and warehouse data reasonably accurate. If warehouse features are used, suppliers are responsible for stock movements, warehouse assignments, routing, and fulfillment decisions.

## 6. Order Fulfillment

Suppliers are responsible for accepting, rejecting, preparing, dispatching, delivering, replacing, or cancelling orders according to agreed commercial terms.

Suppliers must not mark an order delivered, completed, or paid unless the status is accurate.

## 7. Delivery and Drivers

Suppliers are responsible for drivers, delivery staff, vehicles, proof of delivery, failed deliveries, reschedules, delivery conduct, route planning, delivery compliance, and customer communication.

Driver accounts may be restricted to assigned deliveries and monitored through audit logs.

## 8. Invoices and Payments

Suppliers are responsible for invoices, tax compliance, payment terms, payment collection, receipts, credit notes, and financial records. Supplify tools are operational aids and are not a substitute for professional accounting.

## 9. Disputes, Credit Notes, and Replacements

Suppliers must respond to restaurant disputes in good faith. Credit notes, refunds, and replacements recorded in Supplify must be honored according to the applicable commercial agreement.

## 10. Substitutes

Suppliers may propose substitute products where supported. Suppliers must disclose material differences in product, quantity, unit, price, quality, and specifications.

## 11. Deals, Boosts, and Advertising

Suppliers are responsible for the truthfulness and legality of deals. Boosts do not guarantee results. Suppliers must honor live approved deals and must not advertise misleading discounts, unavailable stock, fake prices, or illegal claims.

## 12. Prohibited Products

Suppliers must not list illegal, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, expired, mislabeled, unauthorized, or prohibited products. Supplify may remove products or suspend accounts at its discretion.

## 13. Users and Roles

Suppliers must manage team users, drivers, finance staff, catalog managers, warehouse managers, and branch users responsibly. Suppliers are responsible for actions taken by their users.

## 14. Branches and Add-ons

Supplier branches may require plan limits or paid add-ons. Suppliers must not attempt to bypass branch, warehouse, user, storage, or plan limits.

## 15. Platform Access

Supplify may suspend supplier access for non-payment, abuse, misleading listings, repeated disputes, security issues, legal risk, or violation of the Terms.

## 16. Indemnity

Suppliers agree to indemnify Supplify for claims related to supplier products, listings, prices, deliveries, invoices, taxes, drivers, deals, disputes, illegal products, or supplier misconduct.
