## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy protects Supplify, restaurants, suppliers, guests, drivers, staff, admins, and the platform from illegal, abusive, fraudulent, unsafe, or harmful activity.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

1. Break the law.
2. Commit fraud.
3. Create fake businesses, fake users, fake orders, fake reviews, or fake deals.
4. Upload malware or harmful files.
5. Attempt unauthorized access.
6. Interfere with platform security or infrastructure.
7. Scrape, reverse engineer, or copy the platform unlawfully.
8. Bypass roles, billing, limits, tenant isolation, or feature gates.
9. Spam users, guests, restaurants, suppliers, or drivers.
10. Harass, threaten, discriminate, or abuse others.
11. Upload content you do not have rights to use.
12. Mislead users about prices, discounts, products, stock, delivery, or identity.
13. Manipulate metrics, boost performance, visibility, or analytics.
14. Share login accounts improperly.
15. Use Supplify to transmit illegal or harmful content.

## 3. Prohibited Products and Content

Suppliers must not list or promote products that are illegal, counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, expired, mislabeled, unauthorized, fraudulent, harmful, or prohibited by Supplify.

Products involving special regulation may require proof of authorization, licensing, or compliance.

## 4. Promotional Abuse

Suppliers must not create fake discounts, fake urgency, misleading prices, unavailable deals, false stock, or false performance claims. Supplify may remove or reject any misleading promotion.

## 5. Messaging Abuse

Users must not abuse chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, push notifications, reminders, or public booking communications. Marketing messages must comply with applicable laws and consent requirements.

## 6. Security Abuse

You must not test, scan, attack, overload, or exploit Supplify systems without written authorization.

## 7. Enforcement

Supplify may remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, block users, revoke boosts, cancel deals, disable notifications, lock tenants, terminate accounts, or report conduct to authorities.

## 8. Reporting

Report abuse to [Support Email].
