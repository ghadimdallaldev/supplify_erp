## 1. Scope

This Restaurant Agreement applies to restaurants using Supplify to browse suppliers, place orders, manage receiving, disputes, invoices, reservations, waitlists, branches, staff, and related operations.

## 2. Restaurant Status

The restaurant is an independent business. Supplify provides software tools and is not responsible for operating the restaurant or managing its guests unless expressly agreed in writing.

## 3. Ordering Responsibilities

Restaurants must review supplier products, prices, quantities, units, delivery details, notes, branches, and payment terms before placing orders.

A restaurant is responsible for orders submitted by its authorized users.

## 4. Receiving and Disputes

Restaurants should inspect delivered goods promptly and record receiving results accurately. Missing, damaged, incorrect, or disputed goods should be reported through Supplify within a reasonable time.

False or abusive disputes may result in restrictions.

## 5. Payments and Invoices

Restaurants are responsible for paying suppliers according to agreed terms. Supplify invoice and payment tools are operational records unless connected to an actual payment provider.

## 6. Reservations and Public Booking

Restaurants are responsible for public booking settings, operating hours, table capacity, reservation confirmations, cancellations, waitlists, guest communications, and honoring bookings.

Supplify is not responsible for guest no-shows, overbooking caused by incorrect settings, or restaurant failure to honor reservations.

## 7. Guest Data

Restaurants must use guest data responsibly and only for lawful booking, communication, hospitality, and operational purposes. Restaurants must not misuse guest phone numbers, emails, notes, or preferences.

## 8. Branches and Staff

Restaurants are responsible for branch data, users, roles, permissions, and staff actions. Branch creation may be limited by plan or add-ons.

## 9. Supplier Relationships

Restaurants are responsible for selecting suppliers, verifying supplier suitability, and resolving commercial issues with suppliers.

## 10. Deals and Redemptions

Restaurants may access supplier deals where available. Restaurants must follow deal terms, expiry dates, quantities, and supplier conditions.

## 11. Subscription and Access

Restaurant access may depend on subscription status, Free Trial expiry, plan limits, add-ons, and billing. Supplify may lock creation/update actions for expired or unpaid accounts.

## 12. Indemnity

Restaurants agree to indemnify Supplify for claims related to restaurant orders, guest management, reservations, payments, disputes, staff actions, privacy violations, or misuse of the platform.
