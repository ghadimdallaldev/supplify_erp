## 1. Purpose

This Cookie Policy explains how Supplify uses cookies, local storage, session storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies.

## 2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on a device. They help websites and apps remember sessions, preferences, security states, and usage activity.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential cookies

Used for login, authentication, tenant context, branch switching, security, CSRF protection, session continuity, and core platform functionality.

### Preference cookies

Used to remember language, UI preferences, selected tenant, selected branch, or display choices.

### Analytics cookies

Used to understand app usage, performance, errors, and product improvement. These may be disabled where settings allow.

### Marketing cookies

May be used for marketing attribution or campaigns if enabled. Supplify should not use marketing cookies without appropriate notice and consent where required.

### Mobile SDK identifiers

The mobile app may use device identifiers or SDK storage for push notifications, diagnostics, analytics, and app performance.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services may set cookies or identifiers for authentication, analytics, payments, support, or embedded tools.

## 5. Managing Cookies

Users can manage cookies in browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent Supplify from working correctly.

Mobile app permissions can be managed through device settings.

## 6. Changes

Supplify may update this Cookie Policy as technologies or providers change.
