## 1. Scope

These Terms apply to supplier deals, discounts, promotions, boost packages, paid visibility, admin approval, performance insights, and related promotional tools in Supplify.

## 2. Deal Creation

Suppliers may create deals subject to plan limits, feature gates, admin approval, and platform rules. A deal may require a selected boost package before submission.

## 3. Admin Approval

Supplify may review and approve or reject deal content and boost requests. Approval does not waive supplier responsibility for accuracy, legality, or fulfillment.

Supplify may reject deals that are misleading, unlawful, abusive, unavailable, incorrectly priced, impossible to honor, unsafe, offensive, fraudulent, or inconsistent with platform rules.

## 4. Visibility and Go-Live

Restaurants should only see deals that are approved and live according to the platform’s publishing rules. Pending, rejected, expired, unpaid, paused, or draft deals should not be visible to restaurants.

## 5. Boost Packages

Boost packages may include duration, price, package name, visibility label, placement, and optional performance insights. Prices and durations may change for future deals.

A supplier’s paid or submitted boost should snapshot price and duration so later package changes do not alter an already purchased boost.

## 6. No Guaranteed Results

Boosts may increase visibility but Supplify does not guarantee impressions, clicks, redemptions, orders, revenue, ranking, return on investment, or any business result.

Any estimated reach, visibility label, or recommendation is informational only.

## 7. Supplier Obligations

Suppliers must ensure that all deal prices, quantities, expiry dates, stock, discount claims, terms, and product details are accurate and honored.

Suppliers must not create fake discounts, misleading urgency, unavailable promotions, or false claims.

## 8. Expiry, Pausing, and Removal

Deals may expire after the boost period or deal period. Supplify may pause or remove deals for policy, legal, payment, technical, stock, or abuse reasons.

## 9. Refunds and Credits

Unless required by law or expressly agreed by Supplify, paid boosts are non-refundable once live. If a deal is rejected before going live, Supplify may provide a refund, credit, or resubmission option depending on the situation.

## 10. Performance Insights

Boost insights may include views, clicks, redemptions, orders, or estimated performance. Insights may be delayed, approximate, incomplete, or affected by tracking limitations.

## 11. Restaurant Use of Deals

Restaurants must follow deal terms, expiry dates, quantity limits, and supplier conditions. A deal may not be combined with other discounts unless stated.

## 12. Enforcement

Supplify may suspend deal access, remove deals, disable boosts, restrict suppliers, or require corrections where platform rules are violated.
