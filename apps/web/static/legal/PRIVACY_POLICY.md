## 1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Supplify collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data and business data when users access Supplify through the web app, mobile app, public reservation pages, supplier tools, restaurant tools, driver views, admin tools, APIs, and notifications.

## 2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

### Account and identity data

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Job title or role
- Business name
- Tenant type
- Login/authentication identifiers
- User role and permissions
- Invitation status

### Business and tenant data

- Restaurant or supplier profile
- Branches, warehouses, delivery zones
- Staff and driver records
- Product catalogs
- Product images
- Prices and price lists
- Orders and order items
- Invoices, credit notes, payments, receivables
- Deals, boosts, promotions, and performance data
- Inventory and stock records
- Reservation and waitlist records
- Disputes and replacement records

### Guest reservation data

- Guest name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Party size
- Reservation date/time
- Notes and preferences
- Cancellation/reschedule data
- Public token or waitlist offer token

### Driver and delivery data

- Driver name/contact details
- Assigned orders/routes
- Delivery statuses
- Notes and proof of delivery
- Timestamps
- Location data if enabled in the future

### Communication data

- Chat messages
- In-app notifications
- Email/WhatsApp/SMS/push notification metadata
- Support messages
- Reminder drafts

### Technical and usage data

- IP address
- Browser/device data
- Session identifiers
- App version
- Device permissions
- Logs and errors
- API usage
- Feature usage
- Cookies and similar technologies

### Uploaded files

- Product images
- Logos
- CSV/import files
- Invoices/documents
- Proof-of-delivery files
- Receiving photos
- Other uploaded documents

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to:

1. Provide Supplify services.
2. Authenticate users.
3. Manage tenants, roles, permissions, branches, and warehouses.
4. Process orders, invoices, disputes, reservations, deliveries, and deals.
5. Send operational notifications.
6. Provide customer support.
7. Debug and secure the platform.
8. Enforce billing, subscriptions, limits, and add-ons.
9. Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
10. Improve product features and analytics.
11. Generate aggregated or anonymized insights.
12. Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal and Business Basis

Depending on the situation, we process data because it is necessary to provide the service, perform contracts, support legitimate business operations, comply with legal obligations, protect security, or because a user or tenant has provided appropriate authorization or consent.

## 5. Public Booking and Guest Data

Restaurants using public booking pages are responsible for informing guests about how their data is used and for honoring privacy obligations. Supplify processes guest reservation and waitlist data to provide booking, confirmation, management, waitlist, and notification functions.

## 6. Notifications

We may send in-app, email, WhatsApp, SMS, and push notifications. Notification delivery may require sharing contact details and message content with third-party providers.

Users may manage certain notification preferences in their account or device settings. Some operational or security notifications may be required.

## 7. Mobile App Data

The mobile app may request device permissions for push notifications, camera/photos, file upload, and location if location-based features are enabled. You can manage device permissions through your device settings.

If driver tracking or live location is added, Supplify will provide additional disclosures and may require explicit permission.

## 8. AI-Assisted Features

Supplify may process text, messages, product names, orders, and files through AI-assisted tools for parsing, matching, drafting, or insight generation. Users must review AI output before business action. AI providers may process data as subprocessors where enabled.

## 9. Admin Access and Impersonation

Authorized Supplify personnel may access tenant data or impersonate tenant accounts for support, debugging, billing, security, and compliance. Such access may be logged.

## 10. How We Share Data

We may share data with:

1. Other users in the same tenant based on roles.
2. Restaurants and suppliers involved in an order, invoice, dispute, deal, delivery, or communication.
3. Public guests where necessary for reservation management.
4. Third-party service providers.
5. Payment providers if payments are enabled.
6. Hosting, storage, email, WhatsApp/SMS, push, analytics, authentication, support, AI, and monitoring providers.
7. Legal authorities where required.
8. Business successors in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

## 11. Subprocessors

Supplify may use subprocessors for hosting, storage, messaging, payments, analytics, authentication, monitoring, support, and AI. A list of subprocessors may be provided upon request or published separately.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain data as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain backups, prevent fraud, support audits, and operate the platform.

Retention periods may vary by data type. Account closure does not necessarily mean immediate deletion.

## 13. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as authentication, access controls, role permissions, logging, encryption where appropriate, backups, monitoring, and administrative controls.

No system is completely secure. Tenants are responsible for managing users, passwords, devices, and permissions.

## 14. User and Tenant Rights

Subject to applicable law, users or tenants may request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction of certain data. Some requests may need to be made through the tenant owner or may be limited due to legal, security, billing, audit, or technical reasons.

## 15. International Transfers

Supplify and its subprocessors may process or store data in countries outside Lebanon. Where required, Supplify will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 16. Children

Supplify is intended for business use and is not directed to children. Public reservation pages should not be used by children without appropriate adult involvement.

## 17. Cookies

Supplify uses cookies and similar technologies for sessions, security, preferences, analytics, and performance. See the Cookie Policy for details.
