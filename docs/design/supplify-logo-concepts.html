Recommendation
Concept A is consistent with the current sidebar mark and works great as a favicon + small icon.
Concept C is the boldest and most readable at all sizes — best for marketing and app stores.
Concept B is most distinctive and tells the supply chain story — best for the landing page hero.
To use in the app, copy the SVG code from the mark you want and replace the hexagon SVG in Sidebar.tsx.
