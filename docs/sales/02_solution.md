# 02 — The Solution

## One Platform for Ordering, Receiving, and Paying

Supplify connects **restaurants** and **suppliers** in a single, role-based platform so both sides get clarity and control.

### What we do

- **Unified ordering** — Restaurants build carts from supplier catalogs, place orders, and track status. Suppliers see orders in one place and fulfill with clear workflows.
- **Visible inventory** — Restaurants manage par levels and see what they have; suppliers manage stock and warehouses. Limits and features scale with plan.
- **Receiving and quality** — Restaurants record what arrived and how it looks; suppliers get feedback. Fewer disputes, better relationships.
- **Finance that ties in** — Invoices link to orders and receiving. Restaurants see what they owe; suppliers track payments and overdue balances.
- **Chat in context** — Conversations live next to orders and products, so questions get answered without switching tools.

### Why it matters to restaurants

- Less time on ordering and follow-up; more time on service and operations.
- One place to see orders, inventory, and supplier conversations.
- Clear path from order → delivery → receiving → payment.

### Why it matters to suppliers

- All orders in one system; fewer missed or duplicate orders.
- Better visibility into who orders what and how to prioritize.
- Invoicing and payment tracking built in, so cash flow is easier to manage.

### Outcome

Restaurants and suppliers spend less time on coordination and more on what actually grows their business. The platform scales from a single location to multiple branches and warehouses, with plans and controls that match how serious they are about using it.
