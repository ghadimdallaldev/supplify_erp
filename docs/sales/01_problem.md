# 01 — The Problem

## Why Restaurants and Suppliers Need a Better Way

### Restaurants face daily friction

- **Manual ordering** — Spreadsheets, phone calls, and emails with multiple suppliers waste time and create errors.
- **No single view** — Inventory, orders, and supplier communication live in different tools. Staff can’t see the full picture.
- **Receiving and invoicing don’t connect** — What was ordered, what arrived, and what’s owed are hard to reconcile.
- **Scaling is painful** — Adding locations or suppliers means more manual work, not a clearer process.

### Suppliers face operational strain

- **Order chaos** — Orders arrive by email, phone, and portal. Consolidating and fulfilling them is error‑prone.
- **Limited visibility** — Hard to see which restaurants order what, how often, and how to prioritize.
- **Invoicing and payments** — Tracking who paid what and following up on late payers takes time away from growing the business.

### The cost of “good enough”

- Wasted hours every week on repetitive ordering and reconciliation.
- Lost sales from stockouts or slow reordering.
- Damaged relationships when orders or invoices are wrong or late.
- Difficulty scaling without adding headcount or complexity.

**Bottom line:** Restaurants and suppliers need one place to order, receive, communicate, and pay—so they can focus on running their business, not shuffling spreadsheets.
